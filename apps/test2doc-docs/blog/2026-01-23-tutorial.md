As [foretold in the prophecy billions of years ago at the dawn of time](./2025-12-08-start-of-tutorial.md), I said I was working on a tutorial to help teach TDD with Playwright, RedwoodSDK, and how to generate documentation with Test2Doc for Docusaurus.

**And I did.** [The tutorial](/docs/tutorial-1) is complete and covers all the basic CRUD operations, best practices around testing, refactoring, and deploying to Cloudflare.
<!-- truncate -->

It was quite challenging. Really forcing me to dig deep and understand my own coding best practices and attempt to explain the how and whys. Despite how challenging it was, it was also extremely enlightening. No better way to learn about yourself than to teach others. I highly recommend that everyone gives it a shot at least once.
